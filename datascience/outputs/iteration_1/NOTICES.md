# Approved notice wording

`responses.py` currently has `TBC_NOTICE_MESSAGE = "TBC"`. These are the approved
strings. They are the scientific framing of the product and should be used verbatim.

| code | message |
|---|---|
| `area_level_information` | KinderPrint describes communities, not individual children. Every figure is an area-level statistic and cannot be applied to any person living there. |
| `association_not_causation` | These are observed associations in area-level data. They do not show that any condition caused any child-development outcome. |
| `investigation_leads` | Results are starting points for investigation, not conclusions. They indicate where a closer look may be warranted. |
| `comparable_not_identical` | Matched communities are similar on the socioeconomic and demographic measures used for matching. They are not identical, and unmeasured differences remain. |
| `area_level_comparison` | Differences between communities are subject to sampling uncertainty. Where a difference is not statistically significant this is stated explicitly. |
